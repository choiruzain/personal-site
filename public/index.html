Profile

I hold a PhD from La Trobe University (2020) on scalable machine learning for large-scale data streams. I teach big data, databases, cloud computing and AI with a hands-on, industry-oriented approach: students build and run real systems, not just read about them.

  • Member of the five-person FIT3003 teaching team that received Monash's 2026 SSC Education Excellence Award (team award).
  • Student satisfaction up to 4.70/5 (La Trobe SFT, CSE3CWA, 2024) and 94/100 (Monash, FIT5137, 2022; faculty average 83).
  • Former Lecturer (Level B, Teaching Focussed) in Data Analytics, La Trobe University.

How I teach

  • Scaffolded projects where students use GitHub, Docker, REST APIs, PostgreSQL and cloud deployment.
  • Oral (interview) assessment, worked exemplars and individual feedback sheets.
  • Container-based labs that reproduce industry workflows on students' own laptops.

Projects
  • polyglot-bigdata-lab: a one-command Docker big data lab (Hadoop, Hive, Spark, Trino and eight databases) so students can run an industry-style platform on their own laptops.
  • greenbasket-bi-case: an original business intelligence and data warehousing teaching case. A fictional Melbourne grocery chain with synthetic multi-source data and realistic data quality issues.
  • edge-stream-dss: online stream learning for decision support over MQTT, monitoring a simulated industrial motor. Applies ideas from my PhD on evolving learners for drifting data streams.
  • This website: hosted on Cloudflare Workers; a small AI assistant is coming soon.
