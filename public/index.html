About

I hold a PhD in Data Science from La Trobe University (2020). I teach big data, databases, cloud computing and AI, with a hands-on, industry-oriented approach: students build and run real systems, not just read about them.

From 2023 to 2025 I was a Lecturer (Level B, Teaching Focussed) in Data Analytics at La Trobe University, Bendigo. I currently teach at Monash University and La Trobe University, and taught at RMIT University in 2026.

Teaching

  • Team award: member of the FIT3003 Business Intelligence and Data Warehousing teaching team that received Monash's 2026 SSC Education Excellence Award.
  • Student feedback: La Trobe SFT overall satisfaction up to 4.70/5 (CSE3CWA, 2024); Monash student satisfaction 94/100 (FIT5137, 2022; faculty average 83).
  • Subject coordination: Cloud-Based Web Applications at La Trobe (2023–2024), about 250 students across Bendigo, Bundoora, Sydney and online.
  • Units taught: Data Processing for Big Data, Cloud Computing and Security, Business Intelligence and Data Warehousing, Advanced Database Design, AI Fundamentals, Data Mining, Deep Learning, NLP, Python Programming.

Research

My PhD and research focus on scalable machine learning for large-scale data streams (evolving fuzzy systems, Spark/MapReduce). Selected work:

  • Evolving large-scale data stream analytics based on scalable PANFIS, Knowledge-Based Systems, 2019.
  • Scalable teacher forcing network for semi-supervised large-scale data streams, Information Sciences, 2021.
  • Automatic construction of multi-layer perceptron network from streaming examples, ACM CIKM, 2019.

Projects

  • polyglot-bigdata-lab: a 13-service Docker big data stack (Spark, Hive, Trino and more) so students can run an industry-style platform on their own laptops.
  • greenbasket-bi-case: an original business intelligence and data warehousing teaching case. A fictional Melbourne grocery chain with synthetic multi-source data (POS, app orders, inventory) and realistic data quality issues.
  • edge-stream-dss: online stream learning for decision support over MQTT, monitoring a simulated industrial motor. Applies ideas from my PhD on evolving learners for drifting data streams.
  • This website: coming soon, a small AI assistant that answers questions about my work.
